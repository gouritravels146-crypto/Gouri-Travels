Terms of Service

Simple terms for booking bus, train and car tours with Gouri Travels.in.

Last updated: November 2026

Bookings

  • Choosing seats on this website holds them for 15 minutes. A booking is confirmed only after we receive your payment and confirm it to you.
  • If payment is not received in time, held seats are released automatically.

Train tickets

Train tickets are confirmed by Indian Railways. A train booking is final only when the railway confirms it and we share your PNR with you.

Cancellations and refunds

Each trip page lists its cancellation policy. Please message us on WhatsApp as early as possible if you need to cancel.

Your account

Please keep your Google account secure. Your e-ticket is linked to the account used to book it.

Changes to a trip

Trip details such as timing or vehicle may change for safety, weather or other reasons. We will inform you on WhatsApp or phone.
